Four checks from the November 2026 snapshot

  • Overall output was flat. Pouët recorded 996 coded releases in January–October 2026 and 1,001 in the same months of 2025.
  • Launch timing showed no clear overall increase. This held after accounting for seasonality, long-term trends, major parties, and the COVID period.
  • Groups active in both years shipped less. Across 238 matched groups, credited output fell about 6%, with shared releases divided among the credited groups.
  • Most of the 4K increase was party-tagged. Of the 32 additional 4K releases, 27 carried a party tag.

Pouët can include AI-assisted releases and has no structured AI-use field in this snapshot. Release counts capture what shipped. Time saved and changes in project scope are outside that measure.

Data & methods

One Pouët production counts once in the all-platform total and once on each platform it tags. The event view uses the five highest-ranked platforms as its combined series. Peaks use complete calendar years. Winter runs from December through February and takes the year it ends. Releases with only a known year appear in annual counts.

Format stacks assign one category per coded release in this order: coded wild, size-limited, demo, intro or dentro, then other. Coded wild uses entries with a qualifying coded type. Size-tag views include all coded entries with that tag.

Party status comes from Pouët competition credits. Event dates mark timing.
