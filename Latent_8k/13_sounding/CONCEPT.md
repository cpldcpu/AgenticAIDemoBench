# SOUNDING — working notes

Concept, before code. What the compo research says wins a PC 8k intro:

- **Dune** (Alcatraz) won Revision 2025 on *atmosphere* and a soundtrack that matches the
  scale of the picture. The runner-up, **The Sheep and The Biker**, got 2nd on *narrative*
  and direction. Assembly 2025: **Wunderlust** won the demo compo on art direction alone.
- The Meteoriks reward *Outstanding Concept* ("bold decisions, novel ideas, extending the
  scene's aesthetic vocabulary") and *Best Direction* ("cohesion, camerawork, atmosphere,
  pacing, story"). Not "most effects".
- The old "How to win a compo" checklist still holds: design over technique, consistent
  look, sync the picture to the music, no ripped looks.

## The piece

**SOUNDING.** Night, fog, open sea. A small boat with one lit lantern. A lighthouse miles
off sweeps its beam through the fog and, pass by pass, finds others: not one boat but
dozens, each with a lantern, all feeling their way. The beam never stops.

Sense of it: a "sounding" is the leadsman's measurement of depth — the old way of knowing
where you are — and also the sound a foghorn makes into nothing. The light does not
rescue anyone. It only keeps the watch.

- **Palette**: blue-black, one cold white beam, one amber flame. No mid-tones. (Nothing
  like the folder.)
- **Light is the subject**: the beam is a real volumetric cone in fog, and it occludes
  against the boat as a silhouette. The water carries a long specular path under each
  light. That is the whole "wow" — one idea, executed hard, instead of twelve effects.
- **Music**: 90 BPM, 3/4 — a slow barcarolle in D minor. Foghorn (sub + fifth), a
  bell-buoy gong, a bowed cello melody, a harp figure. No four-on-the-floor, no arps.
  The beam's 16-second revolution is 8 bars, so the picture and the score share a clock.
- **Arc**: lantern → the beam → the swell → the fleet → the watch. The reveal (there are
  others out there) is the emotional turn; the ending is quiet, not a drop.

## Why it should beat the folder

1. Art direction nobody here has: near-black, silhouette, fog, actual night.
2. A story you can follow with no text: one light looking for others.
3. Craft the folder has not shown: real volumetric scattering, occlusion of the shaft by
   geometry, halation, film grain, a shallow depth of field on the hero boat.
4. A soundtrack with a *tune* and a waltz, not a pad.
