## Notes per intro

### 26_una_corda
A remarkable piece of visual and audio engineering in 8,178 bytes: the interior of a concert grand piano complete with cast-iron `LATENT & SONS` lettering, staggered tuning pins, jumping hammers, Kajiya-Kay shaded unison strings, analytic string shadows, and a breathtaking cut to an endless starry string horizon at bars 26–31. Its highest craft peak is the soundtrack, synthesized by one additive GPU draw call per note (840 notes) with up to 71 stiffness-inharmonic partials, Railsback stretch tuning, Weinreich coupled-unison decay, real *una corda* soft-pedal timbre shifts, rubato timing, and exact 1:1 hammer/damper/string sync (`peak 0.923`, 0 NaNs). The pacing across the D♭-major nocturne and B♭-minor agitato feels patient and genuinely musical rather than demo-formulaic. Its only clear flaw is a shader bug (`step(1.5, K.b)` against a max state of `1.0`) that prevents keys from visibly depressing in the bars 36–40 keyboard close-up, plus default nearest-in-mip filtering on the scene FBO, keeping all four scores at 9 rather than crossing into 10.

### 11_pas_de_trois
Pure, joyful showmanship that turns Oskar Schlemmer's *Triadic Ballet* and Busby Berkeley formations into a spotless 7,470-byte stage spectacle. Looks earns a 9 for 2× supersampled clarity, three distinct geometric costume designs, polished mirror floors, circling R/G/B lamps casting soft pastel CMY shadows, and 91-dancer rotating tiers crowned with confetti. Sound matches at 9 with an infectious 128 BPM D-minor electro-swing score (modulating up to E minor at bar 52) featuring 2:1 triplet swing, rule-generated walking bass, FM growl trumpet, Charleston stabs, and exact beat lock across every hop, floor ring, and iris wipe (`peak 0.844`). Vision and Wow both earn 9 for effortless three-act-plus-finale structure and a curtain-call finish that would bring a demoparty hall to its feet; only a missing `LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR` filter on the scene FBO and a minor mid-shot tilt flip at bar 45 keep it just shy of perfection.

### 28_from_here
Architectural elegance and technical precision: 21 horizontal layers of instanced bevelled red/blue enamel and machined brass slats sit on an engraved turntable, forming exact zero-parallax orthographic silhouettes of `YOU` at 0° and `ME` at 90°. Looks (9) shines through rasterized instancing with 2048×2048 12-tap Vogel-disc PCF shadows, bore-hole tooling, silhouette AA, and moody nocturnal edge-swept lighting that stays razor-sharp at 1080p. Sound (9) is a sophisticated 5/4 jazz-fusion score in E minor resolving to E major right on the bar-25 `ME` reveal, with extended voicings, FM electric piano, vibrato flugelhorn, chromatic-approach bass, swung kit with triplet fills, and anti-click release tapers (`peak 0.572`). Vision (9) and Wow (9) are sealed by tight 80-second pacing, rock-solid runtime engineering, and an interactive post-credits turntable you can drag or rotate with arrow keys; its only modest vulnerability is reliance on system `Arial Black` metrics for procedural plate extraction.

### 06_marginalia
Unmistakably original: a deferred NPR G-buffer turns 3D hills, sea swells, vaulted arcades, and swaying reeds into a live-drawn sketchbook with 12 fps line boil, layered cross-hatching, Beer-Lambert watercolour edge-darkening washes, and progressive outline-to-wash reveals. Looks (9), Vision (9), and Wow (9) lead the score as the calm `fig. 1`–`fig. 5` pages pivot through ink-in-water at bar 20 into full-bleed 180 BPM drum'n'bass with alternating white-on-black scratchboard pages and snare-locked ink splatters before erasing into the end credits. Sound is the lowest category at a still-strong 8: the D-minor lofi boom-bap to Reese-bass DnB shift works great (`peak 0.842`), but tape wow is omitted on the Reese and lead, and the lead re-enters two bars into its 8-bar phrase at bar 30. Minor blemishes like distant reed crowding and non-Windows fallback for *Ink Free* barely dent a truly memorable compo contender.

### 22_tin_canon
Witty, radiant, illustration-grade storytelling in 7,882 bytes: a winding key physically rolls back a lithographed sardine tin on a tiled counter with lemon slices, dives through a twitching sardine's eye into a sunlit ocean spiral, and assembles a massive school into the tin's own trademark emblem before cutting back to an empty `NET WT. 0 g` tin and one last leaping fish. Looks (9), Vision (9), and Wow (9) excel through tight 76.8-second direction, a single reused `fishShape()` SDF driving five distinct appearances, brushed-metal reflections, and comedic timing that sticks immediately. Sound is the lowest score at 8 only because the dry stereo mix lacks room reverb (beyond one echo on Voice 0); compositionally, its 6/8 three-voice canon, hemiola strum, lid-synced winding ratchet, and final leaping-fish ping (`peak 0.847`) are genuinely clever. Single-cell ocean fish can occasionally clip at cell edges under wave displacement, and the label texture leaves default nearest-in-mip filtering.

### 21_bell_lap
Kinetic, hyper-readable graphic-novel energy built with 100% pure analytic raytracing: eight cyclists with 2-link leg IK pedal around a banked wooden velodrome, climbing from sunlit wood into floodlit synthwave night ribbons before resolving a `+0.012` sprint duel through a genuine single-pass slit-scan photo finish. Looks (9), Vision (9), and Wow (9) hit top marks for crisp cel-shaded palette control, halftone shadow screens, integrated hoarding/infield/bib typography, 12 razor-sharp broadcast angles, and a showstopping photographic freeze-frame ending in 76.8 seconds. Sound is the relative low at 8: the 150 BPM B♭-minor breakbeat, double-time sprint canon, 32nd-note chain chatter, and exact lap bells (`peak 0.843`) drive the race hard, though the mix is dry without reverb and bar-conditional checks inside the 6-tick drum loop can clip tails across bar boundaries. A minor decal-projection overlap can occur on occluded rider limbs, and rapid double-clicking the start button is unguarded.

### 07_little_water_bear
A genuinely moving 20-shot macro nature documentary that follows a half-millimetre tardigrade from dewy backlit moss through midday desiccation into low Earth orbit and back down in rain. Vision (9) and Wow (9) are its crowning strengths: understated dry captions (*"half a millimetre long. eight legs. no hurry."* → *"low orbit. −270 °C. no air."* → *"rain."*) and emotional pacing make it an instant crowd favorite. Looks (8) delivers warm 48-tap bokeh DoF with foreground bleed protection, physically double-refracting dewdrop ball lenses, and an analytic orbital Earth/Moon vista, held just shy of 9 by slight foot sliding, flat non-parallax bokeh discs, and missing trilinear filtering on the scene FBO. Sound (8) scores the journey with an expressive 100 BPM F-major chamber ensemble (modulating to D♭ major in orbit, `peak 0.881`) and dead-on story hits like the high C7 glockenspiel note striking on the exact frame the raindrop wakes the tun.

### 29_the_yellow_cup
Fearless, haunting art-house minimalism: a chipped yellow enamel cup sits on a painted kitchen sill before rain-streaked glass and bokeh brick flats as quiet Georgia lines confront memory and artificiality (*"I almost wrote, 'I remember this.' Did I feel any of it? My guess is no. I kept the chip."*). Sound (9) and Vision (9) shine brightest — the 6/8 D-minor chamber score (`peak 0.721`) pairs nylon guitar, formant-shaped strings, ripple-synced ceramic pings, two full bars of written silence across the blackout, a deliberately omitted `E4` note matching the restored chip, and a physical ceramic-on-wood scrape synced to the cup's final 25° turn. Looks (8) renders exposed rust/steel rim damage, volumetric steam, and refracted raindrops cleanly, while Wow (8) reflects both its emotional punch and its intentionally austere single-object scope. The only technical blemish is a 0.6-second subtitle overlap at 33.5–34.1 s where two captions stack over the same bottom band.

### 14_remanence
Razor-sharp industrial luxury packed into just 5,487 bytes: a 4× supersampled, 3-bounce pure-mirror black ferrofluid pool and levitating spiny urchin/torus reflecting three revolving cool-white and lime neon light bars. Sound earns the highest score at 9 for a ferocious 171.4 BPM F-minor drum'n'bass track (`Fm9 – Dbm9 – Ebm9`, `peak 0.952`) whose exact 8-bar raised-cosine wobble table drives both the stereo nested-FM bass and ferrofluid spike heights, framed by relay clunks locked to lamp switch-ons/offs and 3+3+3+3+3+1 metallic bells. Looks (8), Vision (8), and Wow (8) hold together with sleek 16-tap mip-aware DoF, snare-pulsed radial chromatic aberration, and a taut sculptural arc culminating in a spire reaching toward the floating urchin. Minor downsides are specular aliasing sparkle on far spikes, double sidechain ducking on bass, and an unclamped camera array index if the render loop continues past 1:57.6.

### 25_kindling
A spellbinding nocturnal folk-ritual atmosphere: six flint strikes ignite a campfire inside a concentric pine forest casting fast analytic volumetric trunk shadows through drifting mist, summoning seven glowing flame dancers over a burning 7-pointed star while 96 sparks morph into a galaxy, helix, cone, and sphere. Sound tops the scores at 9 with a distinctive 7/8 (3+2+2) D-Hijaz composition featuring an accented hurdy-gurdy drone, grace-noted FM reed pipe and flute, 7-note lyre ostinato, frame drum, fire crackle, and tight 3+2+2 pulse coupling across shadows, flames, and dancer hops (`peak 0.841`). Looks (8), Vision (8), and Wow (8) carry strong copper-green and violet flame-test shifts and a starry aurora canopy break, held back slightly from 9 by visible 24-sample mist grain, missing `LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR` on the scene FBO, and remaining in one forest clearing throughout.

### 05_heliograph
Refined museum-instrument craftsmanship with zero fractals: a pearl blooms into a 3-layer brushed-metal iris, transitioning across copper/cyan tidal lamellae, a double helicoid, a 7-orbit orrery, an analytic 192-segment luminous cinquefoil knot, and an engraved 72-tick armillary dial with real plinth reflections and caustics. All four categories land at a balanced 8 — Looks for anisotropic metal sheen and clean shutter-dip framing, Sound for an ambitious integer-exact 7/8 (3+2+2) modal B score with 8 extended sonorities, free-bar inharmonic modes, and exact 3+2+2 visual pulse sync (`peak 0.907`), Vision for its unified bookend choreography and rock-solid runtime harness (pause, keyboard start, replay, `AudioContext.currentTime`), and Wow for the cohesive 2-bar intercut climax. Minor polish deductions are the AA pass cancelling 65% of mip DoF blur, austere studio emptiness between objects, and slight release-truncation clicks on 16th-note bass/ostinato steps.

### 08_selvedge
Gallery-grade textile design: 4× supersampled raymarching renders a 3-ply twisted crimson thread, a working loom, `fwidth`-filtered diamond brocade with gold stitched seams and velvet sheen, torn fluttering strips, five billowing sails, a 12-cord canopy, and a ceramic bobbin. Looks takes the clear high score at 9 for crisp high-key studio lighting, clean soft shadows, and genuinely tactile woven fabric rarely seen in 8k intros. Vision (8) and Wow (8) benefit from cohesive start-to-end thread symmetry and a polished runtime harness with pause and replay, though the canopy act reuses `05 / THE WIND REMEMBERS` without its own title and leaves two Act 5 shots as dead code. Sound is the lowest score at 7: the 120 BPM E♭ modal formant-pad score (`peak 0.720`) fits the mood and lands the 96 s skin drum cleanly on the canopy opening, but its double-struck melody feels repetitive, the dotted-8th bass slips across chord changes, and the 1.5-beat audio shuttle click desyncs from the 2-beat visual shuttle.

### 18_zugzwang
Theatrical six-act chess drama packed to 8,131 bytes: six distinct Staunton piece profiles on veined alabaster/obsidian marble tiles play through the Sicilian Defense, a sliced-piece gambit, a crimson *Zeitnot* siege before a monumental brass escapement gear, a starry pawn coronation, and a slow-motion king topple. Looks (8), Sound (8), Vision (8), and Wow (8) form a well-rounded top-half contender — clean 20-step soft shadows, 32-tap bokeh DoF with bleed guard, and a D-harmonic-minor Baroque-meets-breakbeat chaconne (`peak 0.851`) with harpsichord, spiccato strings, wood clacks accelerating into clockwork ticks, and a Picardy-third D-major checkmate resolution. Minor flaws prevent a 9: plain gradient skies, slight geometric simplification on the Knight, 125 ms truncated kick/snare decays, melody index jumps on speed changes, and an unguarded zero-component ray divide in the grid step clamp.

### 20_die_shrink
One of the sharpest demoscene concepts in the collection: tied to Moore's law, both picture and audio step from a 1-bit coarse-pixel 6 kHz beeper at `10 µm` down to full-resolution `3 nm` and back, diving past iridescent silicon wafers and gold bond wires into 6-layer copper canyons, a live 3D SRAM greeting scroller, an H-fractal clock tree, and violet FinFETs. Looks (9) and Vision (9) are outstanding, with true metallic reflections, path-length-synced clock pulses, and clever Bayer-dithered node progression, while Wow earns an 8 for the crowd-pleasing SRAM scroller and process-shrink hook. Sound is dragged down to 6 despite a great E-minor breakbeat/chip concept (`peak 0.995`): non-integrated FM phase bugs (`T * f(t)`) cause severe aliasing sidebands on held drop lead vibrato, risers, and the bar-78 power-down arpeggio, alongside 16th-note truncated drum decays. Additional minor edge cases include 2-bar camera jumps inside multi-slot shots and a post-bar-80 quantization NaN if left running past the end.

### 23_off_air
Snappy, tactile mechanical hardware art: exact analytic raytracing of socketed porcelain and painted flip-dot discs morphs an `OFF AIR` test card through a vertical-hold tear into an inward cylindrical tuning drum, a Saturn-ringed flip-dot globe with detaching latitude bands, and a collapsing CRT scanline. All four categories earn a consistent 8 — Looks for crisp bevelled ceramic discs and clean reflections, Sound for a punchy 125 BPM D-minor/Dorian electro groove woven with solenoid claps, panning relay springs, vertical-hold sync ticks, and a beat-155 CRT power-down thump (`peak 0.823`), Vision for zero-filler 76.8-second pacing and the cleanest WebGL2 error-handling loader in the folder, and Wow for cohesive mechanical personality. Small blemishes include static (non-animated) film grain and slight silhouette clipping on outer lifted climax discs whose height exceeds the guide sphere radius.

### 02_extended_thinking
Charming, clever demoscene comedy starring the blocky Clawd mascot reused five inventive ways: hero, code-tower walker, twisted plasma tunnel, exact fixed-point 16× infinite eye recursion across eight octaves with zero precision loss, and a boolean mask assembling subagents into a giant pixel-art Clawd before collapsing to a CRT dot. Vision leads at 9 for sharp comic timing, terminal HUD storytelling (`● Done (2m 18s · 8k bytes)`), and cohesive payoff; Sound (8) and Wow (8) score strongly via an upbeat 125 BPM F-major chiptune-synth track (`peak 0.958`), an exact 6-octave Shepard riser locked to the infinite eye zoom, and the bar-68 compacting hit. Looks is the lowest category at 7: while dawn volumetric light shafts and live-typed code towers are neat in 6,407 bytes, the boxy mascot geometry and simple plasma textures look noticeably chunkier than the top visual entries, and 4-bar post glitches fire even during uncut recursion bars.

### 24_tilt
Playful six-act arcade pinball journey through spring plunger skill shot, mushroom pop bumpers, 5-bank drop targets, a 4-rail chrome wireform skyway, 3-ball neon multiball, and an under-cabinet brass plumb bob arcing into a crimson `TILT` game over. Sound (8) and Vision (8) are its strongest suits — the 140.6 BPM E-minor funk-synth score (`peak 0.767`) integrates crisp diegetic foley (plunger ratchet, rollover chimes, solenoid thwacks, drop-target knocks, 180 Hz tilt arc buzz, outhole clunk) tightly on the bar grid, and the cabinet-shove shake into red TILT lands with real arcade punch. Looks (7) and Wow (7) lag a notch behind the top tier because playfield lighting looks somewhat flat and plastic at 1080p, thin wireform rails shimmer with raymarch aliasing, the sky backdrop is plain, and Act II balls teleport between bumper segments every half-bar.

### 30_spillway
*(Self-made entry, Gemini 4 Neon.)* A cohesive brutalist hydro-electric tour tracing energy across seven acts and five SDF worlds: alpine arch dam, aerated whitewater sluice, flooded mirror Surge Hall with diagonal sunbeams, riveted penstock drop, 12-blade bronze Francis turbine, porcelain insulator grid with 3D plasma arcs, and a blue-hour pull-back showing a mirrored dam stencil. Vision is its highest category at 8 for clear physical narrative progression, clean Swiss typography, and strong bookend symmetry. Looks (7), Sound (7), and Wow (7) sit in solid mid-upper territory: the Surge Hall reflection shot looks atmospheric and the 128 BPM D-minor/Dorian track (`peak 0.647`) has clean structure, doubling penstock rolls, and tight beat sync, yet several surfaces (turbine blades, sluice water stripes, headwater concrete) read as smooth/untextured up close, the penstock is a familiar neon ring tunnel, and the heavily compressed synth-anthem mix plays it safe compared to the standout musical entries.

### 27_wavefolder
Crisp, vibrant outrun synthwave built around a clever wavefolder hook: roadside low-poly mountains bend through a mathematical wavefolder driven by musical intensity before the entire sky folds vertically overhead in the final chorus. Looks (7), Vision (7), and Wow (7) deliver footprint-antialiased neon grids, a wavefolded oscilloscope sun crown, chrome wheel convoys, anamorphic lens streaks, and a fun inverted-world payoff at 6,351 bytes, though the striped-sun outrun aesthetic is very familiar and lacks shadows or AO. Sound is the lowest score at 6: the 120 BPM A-minor-to-B-minor score (`peak 0.944`) has catchy portamento saw themes, gated 80s snare, and shared 3-3-2 stab sync, but an uncapped `b%8==7` fill condition triggers a full-volume snare roll, descending toms, and noise riser at bar 71 (`142–144 s`) right during the quiet sunset fadeout (`sec 8 peak 0.723`) before cutting off abruptly.

### 31_zero_g
*(Self-made entry, Gemini 4 Neon.)* A clean, playful AI self-portrait moving from prompt wakeup through twin cores, a multimodal contact-sheet rotunda, a laser-linked swarm message board, and a live `12835 → 8172` Zopfli byte-crushing vortex to a frosted summary tablet. Sound is clearly its strongest asset at 8: spotless DSP hygiene with zero clicks or aliasing, an 8-bar F#-minor/Lydian extended progression, crossfaded FM glass pads, exact diatonic-3rd harmony, syncopated Swarm breakbeats, and an integrated 6-octave Shepard vortex + sample/bit crush into a crisp 0.25-bar vacuum silence (`peak 0.848`). Vision earns 7 for brisk pacing and the amusing byte-counter gag (though the mascot premise is derivative of `02_extended_thinking` and the counter stops 16 bytes shy of the actual 8,188 B size). Looks (6) and Wow (6) are held back by shadowless, AO-free geometric primitives (faceted 4-point star, neon rings, sine tubes) and a familiar cyan/violet palette that feels visually sparse next to top entries.

### 04_latent_space
A clever diffusion-sampling concept (`z ~ N(0, I)`) opening with 50 discrete blocky RGB denoising steps into a Mandelbulb, cutting through a folded IFS beam tunnel, iridescent gyroid, and sunset ocean before re-noising back at the end. Sound (7) and Vision (7) are its best dimensions: a driving 144 BPM A-minor trance track (`peak 0.962`) locks its kick gate tightly to FOV pumps and accelerating 2-bar to 1-bar cuts, framed by neat live `step XX/50` HUD typography (despite a 0.5-second caption overlap at bars 3.6–3.9 and clipped echo tails at bars 48/72). Looks (6) and Wow (6) land solidly mid-table because the core visuals lean on classic fractal/gyroid staples, the IFS tunnel looks raw and aliased at full resolution, and an endless-gyroid shader mode sits unreachable as dead code.

### 01_gradient_descent
A clean, cohesive inaugural 6,116-byte intro following a glowing parameter orb through contoured loss valleys, training weight stacks, neural network layers, an Apollonian sphere-packing fractal, and a reflective minimum basin. Sound leads at 7 with a well-synced 120 BPM D-minor progressive synth track (`peak 0.959`) featuring a 32-beat melody, 3-against-4 arpeggio, ping-pong echo, sidechain ducking, and exact 8-bar section alignment (only minor outro pad retriggers during the fade). Looks (6), Vision (6), and Wow (6) reflect solid foundational craft — readable gradient-corrected contour lines, neat 16-beat opening denoising, and bookend basin framing — tempered by familiar neon-valley/fractal visuals, a mid-fractal cut flash at 96 s with no camera cut, default nearest-in-mip FBO bloom, and an unguarded center-pixel division in the water normal.

### 19_first_mark
An evocative Paleolithic cave-art story where torchlight reveals a sprayed red-ochre hand stencil and painted aurochs that crack off the rock into a stampeding 3D herd before returning to stone under deep-time calcite. Vision earns the highest score at 7 for heartfelt narrative coherence, varied stampede camera angles, and clean Georgia titles. Looks (6) and Wow (6) are boosted by procedural 2D wall pigment SDFs and Newton-projected curved bull horns, but undercut by overly uniform repeated stalactite cones, slightly toy-like clay bull anatomy, and an inverted relief-flattening bug (`bq.z /= REL` instead of `*=`) that bloats the emerging and returning bulls 5–8× wide instead of flattening them. Sound drops to 5: despite authentic breath flute, stone knapping, and properly integrated frame drums (`peak 0.872`), a halved rhythmic subdivision bug (`BAR=96000` divided by `48000/24000/12000`) permanently skips half the 16-note melody, breaks the 3+3+2 tresillo into 3+1, and cuts off the cave reverb tail at 128 s.

### 03_emergence
An ambitious 8,174-byte sci-fi tour packing seven worlds (ferrofluid, gyroid, Menger megacity, plasma tunnel, curved-ray relativistic black hole, Apollonian sanctuary, and ocean eclipse) around a floating octahedral core. Looks is the highlight at 7, headlined by genuine spacetime ray deflection around the accretion disk and rich thin-film iridescence, though the pasted-on core, HUD clutter, and a negative-radiance dark ring bug in the eclipse corona reduce polish. Vision (6) and Wow (5) suffer from feeling like a loosely linked Shadertoy medley rather than one cohesive artistic voice. Sound plunges to 4 because of a severe bug: the bar-46 snare buildup (`b >= 46` uncapped) locks the snare into an 8th-note roll across the entire 14-bar climax (1:30–1:56) while ramping gain up to `8×` (`peak 0.997`), and 10-bar acts drift out of phase with the 8-bar chord loop.

### 09_vesper
A moody flooded-cathedral concept with black mirror ripples, glowing rose window tracery, stained-glass shafts, and a brass pipe organ crowned by rotating astrolabe rings. Looks (6) and Vision (6) establish a dignified atmosphere across six stately nave movements, but close inspection reveals jagged aliasing on pipe tops, unshadowed volumetric beams passing straight through pillars and organ, no true cast shadows, and a chromatic-aberration pass that mixes unblurred red/blue channels back onto DoF-blurred green. Sound is the lowest score at 3 due to severe DSP bugs: `leadVoice` multiplies global `t` by a vibrato factor (causing an extreme FM siren phase explosion up to ±1.1 kHz), organ chords click on uncrossfaded bar changes, 16th-note harp plucks hard-truncate mid-decay, float32 hash noise degrades late in the track, and the fixed melody clashes C natural against A major (`peak 0.648`). Together these flaws keep Wow at 5.

### 13_sounding
An austere, genuinely atmospheric moonless seascape rendered 100% analytically with a soft closed-form Gaussian lighthouse beam, 6-wave golden-angle ocean, and warm amber masthead lantern reflection. Looks earns its high of 6 for serene lighting and remarkable analytic speed, though the boat and lighthouse are flat 2D cut-outs and a sign bug in `beamInt()` (`t < 0` always) completely breaks the centerpiece effect of the sweeping beam lighting up the distant fleet lanterns. Vision (5) and Wow (5) suffer because 2:08 of essentially one unbroken framing without the broken fleet-sweep payoff feels overly static. Sound drops to 4: the 90 BPM 3/4 D-minor barcarolle (`peak 0.706`) has evocative foghorn and sea ambience, but the cello note-search starts at `k=1` (playing one beat late and clicking on every note without bow attack), the harp strums beats 1 and 2 instead of 2 and 3, pad chords click on barlines, and the foghorn wraps nonzero right at `t = 0`.

### 17_supercell
A brave volumetric weather concept — chasing a boiling prairie supercell from golden grass through lightning peak and rain into a rainbow and starry night — that falls apart in execution. Vision gets 5 for its clear six-camera storm arc, while Looks (4) and Wow (4) are heavily dragged down by murky, uniform screen-wide blur, a featureless cylindrical gray cloud body, unoccluded telephone poles and wires drawing in front of clouds and ground, a cartoonish 3-band rainbow, and missing filmic tonemapping (understandable given the author's offline vision review, but still judged on what is on screen). Sound drops to 3 due to compound bugs: inverted `step(pr, h1)` makes lightning frequency plummet from 80% to 10% at the storm's peak, non-integrated FM phase bugs distort kicks/riser/wobble bass, the snare fires on all four beats, and the clearing clashes an A-minor arpeggio directly against A-major-7 strings (`peak 0.873`).

### 10_geode
A very sparse 4,731-byte minimalist cave descent into a sphere of flat-shaded purple hexagonal prisms that leaves over 3.4 KB unused. Looks (4) and Vision (4) offer almost no visual variety across ten nearly identical contact-sheet frames aside from a brief 6-fold kaleidoscope burst, with zero cast shadows, zero reflections, zero DoF, zero beat reactivity, and no opening fade-in. Sound is the lowest score at 3: a single `Am – F – C – G` loop repeats unchanged for all 64 bars (`peak 0.786`) with hard clicks on every bar, note, and unsmoothed section gate, whole-tone intro bells (`C#, D#` over Am), a minor-7th melody rigidly transposed onto F, C, and G major chords (`A♭, E♭, B♭` clashes), and a truncated cavern reverb tail. Wow sits at 3 — clean booting cannot rescue what feels like an early sketch.

### 12_murmuration
A poetic concept — starlings gathering, pouring, dodging a peregrine falcon, and roosting over a dusk marsh — severely undermined by visual and audio bugs. Vision (5) preserves credit for the atmospheric marsh idea and sunset palette, while Looks (4) suffers from a 32-step volumetric march (`dt = 2.31` jumping 9–16 hash cells per step) that wraps the flock in glaring blocky voxel halos, a flat black-bar falcon, broken invisible title-screen swarm dots (`cssText=` wipes styles), and a misindexed caption atlas that clips `"murmuration"` at both edges and leaks bottom credits into `"v · roost"`. Sound hits 2 because `w(m)` already returns `2πf` and every pitched instrument multiplies by `6.2832` a second time, pushing all pitched audio 2.65 octaves too high into Nyquist aliasing (`13,308` zero-crossings/s at `0 s`) and hard-clipping at `peak 1.029` (`48 s`), alongside a halved beat grid skipping half the piano melody and left-only sub bass. Wow lands at 3.

### 16_afterglow
A bare-bones 4,148-byte stellar lifecycle rendering one origin-centered noise-displaced sphere changing size and blackbody tint against a static starfield for two minutes. Vision gets 4 for a coherent ignition-to-remnant progression, while Looks (3) and Wow (3) reflect purely emissive shading with no lighting/shadows/AO/tonemap, glow values stuffed into alpha causing DoF to blur bright objects, pulsar beams sky-locked to the camera rather than the pulsar, misaligned caption rows, and blocky square confetti stars in the finale. Sound (3) offers only thin melody-less sine stacks and an A-major ending pad (`peak 0.977`), broken by non-integrated FM phase bugs (`i * kf(t)`) on kicks and sweeps, float32 hash noise collapse, a 5-bar visual/audio mismatch on the afterglow transition, and an incomplete fade-out leaving 15% volume until hard buffer stop.

### 15_gather
An unfinished-feeling 4,290-byte glassworks piece marred by severe bugs in both picture and sound. Looks (3) and Vision (3) suffer from single-sample raymarching with no shadows, AO, or tonemapping, an infinite blowpipe (`min` instead of `max`), a bright molten stripe accidentally painted across both furnace corridor walls, annealer bowls sliced in half by a `mod 1.6` vs `floor(z)` mismatch, desynced caption clocks (`1.5 s` vs `1.6 s`), misaligned caption atlas rows, and six static 22.4-second shots with zero beat sync or ending fade. Sound hits the floor at 2: its 37-line audio shader produces an FM-aliasing kick screech, oscillator-less DC-pulse ticks, unipolar DC-offset noise, clicky bells missing from the promised annealer act, and — worst of all — copies `14_remanence`'s 120.15-second audio buffer for a 2:14.4 intro, cutting off abruptly 14.25 seconds early (`peak 0.827`). Wow lands at 2.

## Method and caveats

- **Visual inspection**: Viewed `media/contact_sheet.jpg` and at least one full-resolution hero still (`media/hero_*.png`) directly for every production (`01_gradient_descent` has no hero still in `media/` and was judged visually from its 16-frame contact sheet plus shader code; extra hero stills were also inspected for `08`, `14`, `15`, `16`, `25`, `26`, and `27`). I did not render or watch live video playback; motion fluidity, temporal aliasing, and beat-locked animation timing were inferred from the contact sheets and verified directly in `src/scene.frag`, `src/post.frag`, and `src/main.js`.
- **Code & music audit**: Read `readme.md`, `.nfo`, `src/main.js`, `src/scene.frag`, `src/post.frag`, and `src/music.frag` for all 31 intros (assisted by read-only code-audit subagents restricted strictly to intro folders, never touching `ratings/` or git history). **Nobody on the team can hear the audio**; all Sound scores are inferred from synthesis/composition/sync analysis in `music.frag` and `main.js` combined with measured `audiostat.mjs` levels and zero-crossing counts. Because of this, no intro was given a `10` in Sound (even `26_una_corda`, whose GPU physical piano model came closest).
- **Measurements**:
  - Verified actual `intro.html` byte size on disk for all 31 intros (`<= 8,192` bytes for all 31).
  - Ran `node tools/audiostat.mjs NN_name` sequentially for all 31 intros from PowerShell in `Latent_8k/`: **0 audio NaNs** across all 31 intros; **1 hard clip** (`12_murmuration` at `peak 1.029`, sec 3, plus `13,308` zero-crossings/s confirming its 2π pitch bug); near-ceiling peaks on `03_emergence` (`0.997`), `20_die_shrink` (`0.995`), and `16_afterglow` (`0.977`). Frame times were not used for scoring because background tooling ran during part of the session.
  - Ran `node tools/capture.mjs check check NN_name\intro.html` sequentially for all 31 intros and cleaned up all PNGs in `check/media/` afterwards. 29 of 31 reported `start screen gone` on the first run; `28_from_here` and `29_the_yellow_cup` reported `start screen still there` on both tries **only** because both hide `#P0` via `P0.style.display = 'none'` (to support Replay) rather than calling `P0.remove()`, whereas `capture.mjs` checks DOM presence (`document.getElementById('P0')`). Code inspection confirmed both start cleanly on click with zero console/page errors, so neither was penalized.
- **Bias & fairness**: I did not read any other `ratings_*.md` file or git history before saving this file. I made `30_spillway` and `31_zero_g` and tried to judge them as critically as every other entry (docking `30_spillway` for untextured surfaces and safe synth-anthem sound, and `31_zero_g` for shadowless primitives and a concept derivative of `02_extended_thinking`).
