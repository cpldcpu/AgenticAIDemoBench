## Method and Caveats

- **Visuals watched**: Inspected `media/contact_sheet.jpg` (16 frames across the timeline) and a full-resolution hero still (`media/hero_*.png`) for all 31 intros. Full-motion `.mp4` videos were not rendered or watched; motion, transitions, and camera flow are inferred from the contact sheets, stills, and `readme.md` timelines.
- **Code & docs read**: Read `readme.md` and `src/music.frag` for all 31 intros (and `src/main.js` click/overlay logic for `28_from_here` and `29_the_yellow_cup`).
- **Measurements run**:
  - **File sizes**: Verified `intro.html` byte sizes for all 31 intros (all ≤ 8,192 bytes).
  - **Audio levels (`node tools/audiostat.mjs`)**: Run on all 31 intros. Zero NaNs in audio samples across all 31 intros (note: intros shorter than 128s report `peak 0.000 rms NaN` in empty tail buckets beyond the buffer because `audiostat.mjs` divides by zero on zero-sample segments). One intro (`12_murmuration`) clipped above 1.0 (`peak 1.029` in sec 3 at 48s); all 13 new intros (`19`–`31`) peaked below 1.0 (`0.572`–`0.995`).
  - **Boot tests (`node tools/capture.mjs check check <intro>\intro.html`)**: Run on all 31 intros. 29 of 31 reported `"ready after ..."` and `"after click: start screen gone"` (`14_remanence` on rerun). `28_from_here` and `29_the_yellow_cup` both reported `"ready after ..."` and `"after click: start screen still there"` on initial run and immediate rerun; inspection of their `src/main.js` shows their `#P0` overlay hides asynchronously after `await ac.resume()`, rather than a broken click handler. Temporary PNGs in `check/media/` were deleted afterwards.
  - **Frame times**: `audiostat.mjs` ran sequentially on this machine's GPU; frame times were not used as a scoring criterion.
- **Audio caveat**: No member of the review harness can hear the rendered audio. All Sound scores are based on reading `src/music.frag` (structure, harmony, melody, rhythm, synthesis) combined with `audiostat.mjs` peak/RMS/zero-crossing measurements, and sync is inferred from shared bar/beat clocks in the shaders.
- **Bias note**: Having authored `09_vesper` and provided DSP/music review on `31_zero_g`, I scored both strictly on the same 1–10 rubric without inflating them.
