## Method and caveats

I inspected each available contact sheet and Pou?t still, and skimmed the scene, post-processing and music shader comments and the relevant readme descriptions. The contact sheets are sampled stills, not playback; motion, transitions and synchronization are inferred from the images and source, not watched. I cannot hear the audio: Sound scores are necessarily uncertain estimates of the written composition and synthesis, not judgments of audible timbre or mix. All release sizes were measured on disk. I ran audiostat and the packed-release boot check for all 31. All reached the ready screen; 29 reported the start screen gone. The checker reported it still present for 28 and 29 on repeat, but their post-click screenshots show the running scenes: their source hides the P0 element, while the checker tests only whether it exists. No audio samples were reported as NaN. The only reported peak above 1.0 was 12_murmuration at 1.029 in the 48–64 second section; RMS NaNs printed for empty sections beyond some tracks are not sample NaNs. Frame timings were not scored because GPU idleness was not established. I did not read other ratings or git history.
